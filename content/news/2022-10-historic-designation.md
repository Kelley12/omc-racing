---
title: OMC Track Receives National Historic Designation
date: "2022-10-15"
author: OMC Board
excerpt: The Owyhee Motorcycle Club track has been officially added to the National Register of Historic Places, recognizing its legacy since 1940.
---

We are proud to announce that the Owyhee Motorcycle Club track has been added to the **National Register of Historic Places** through a joint effort with Preservation Idaho.

The track has hosted continuous motorcycle events since the club's founding in 1940, making it one of the longest-running motorsports venues in the Pacific Northwest. This historic designation recognizes the track's cultural and recreational significance to the region.

This designation does not change how we operate — members and guests can still enjoy the facility as they always have. It does, however, protect the land from future incompatible development and acknowledges what the OMC community has known for decades: this place is special.

Thank you to everyone in the OMC community whose dedication over 80+ years made this recognition possible.
